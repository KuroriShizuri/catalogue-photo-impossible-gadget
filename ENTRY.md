# The TOAST²: A Toaster That Toasts Toasters

The TOAST² is an impossible gadget—a sleek white and chrome toaster whose absurd job is toasting other toasters. This studio catalogue photo captures the moment a miniature chrome toaster pops out of the main unit's glowing slot, mid-use and steaming. A single untouched slice of bread rests on a small white plate beside it, the unit's only accessory, completing the surreal tableaux of recursive toasting.
