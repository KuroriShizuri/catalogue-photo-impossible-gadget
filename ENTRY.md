# ENTRY.md — The Catalogue Photo of an Impossible Gadget

The gadget is the TOAST²: a sleek white-and-chrome four-slot toaster whose absurd job is toasting other toasters — caught mid-use, a miniature chrome toaster pops out of its glowing slot, steaming, with a slice of toast on top.

Its one accessory is a single untouched slice of white bread on a small white plate beside it, waiting its turn and sharpening the joke: this toaster has no interest in bread.
